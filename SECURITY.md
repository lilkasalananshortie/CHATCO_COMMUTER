| ------- | ------------------ |
| 1.0.x   | :white_check_mark: |
| < 1.0.0 | :x:                |

---

## Reporting a Vulnerability

The ChatCo Development and Operations Team takes the security of our commuters, transport cooperatives, and data seriously. If you discover a vulnerability, please report it privately:

1. **Email**: Contact us at **`security@chatco.online`** (or cc: `support@chatco.online`).
2. **Details to Include**:
   * Type of vulnerability (e.g., Auth bypass, Information disclosure, Insecure storage).
   * Exact steps or proof-of-concept (PoC) to reproduce the issue.
   * Device model, OS version, and app version (e.g., Android 14, ChatCo Commuter v1.0.0).
   * Any potential impact you have identified.
3. **Response Timelines**:
   * **Acknowledgment**: Within 24 hours.
   * **Initial Triage & Assessment**: Within 48 hours.
   * **Status Updates**: Every 72 hours until patch release.
4. **Public Disclosure**:
   * We kindly request that you do not publicly disclose the issue until our team has tested and deployed a patch to protect our commuters and transport personnel.

## On-Call & Security Incident Protocol

* **Primary Security Officer**: Lead Systems Administrator (`security@chatco.online`)
* **Incident Escalation**: High-severity vulnerabilities and data breach reports trigger the immediate escalation protocol defined in [`docs/INCIDENT_RUNBOOK.md`](docs/INCIDENT_RUNBOOK.md).
